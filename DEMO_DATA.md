## Demo scheduling
Both placeholder doctors have a sample Sunday–Saturday schedule of 9:00 AM–5:00 PM with a 1:00–2:00 PM break. The booking engine uses this schedule and service duration to calculate slots.

## What to demonstrate to a client
1. Public website and mobile navigation.
2. Doctor profiles and service pages.
3. Appointment date → doctor → service → live available slots.
4. Appointment request being stored in Supabase.
5. Admin dashboard with pending/confirmed appointments.
6. Status changes and audit history.
7. Admin-only doctor/service management.
8. Doctor-specific appointment visibility.
9. Double-booking prevention.
